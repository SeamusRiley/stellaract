## Post-Frame

Post-frame construction is the honest way to put up a working building. Posts in the ground carry the load, so you get wide clear spans with less material, fewer pieces, and a frame that goes up fast. For shops, sheds, and work rooms it is the structure we reach for first: engineered to the site, and built to be maintained, not just to be new.

## Design-Build

Stellaract designs what it builds and builds what it designs. One contract, one point of responsibility, and the person who drew your building is the person standing in it with you at the end. Nothing is lost between a designer's intentions and a builder's shortcuts, because they are the same hands.

## Finishes

A finish is where a building meets the hand and the weather, so we choose finishes on the real material, in real light, before anything is ordered. Stains and sealers are tested on the actual boards, colors are judged after they cure, and everything is specified for how it will age outdoors, not how it looks in the can.

## Ella's Rule

Everything you create at this company must be of the quality that you would make something if you were making it for the love of your life—the most adored person in your world. Whether your Ella is a crush, a partner, a daughter, or son, let that which you create here be a gift from the highest within you to the highest thing on Earth.

*At Stellaract, Ella is at the center of all we do.*
